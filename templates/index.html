Process

Three steps from image to prediction

01 — UPLOAD

Select a thermal image

Choose an exported thermal image file from your device. Raw radiometric temperature files may require a separate conversion step.

02 — INFER

ResNet50 analyzes it

The Flask backend resizes the image to 224 × 224 pixels and applies the ResNet50 preprocessing used by the application.

03 — REVIEW

Review the class score

See the predicted class and model score. This research prototype should not replace field diagnosis or expert advice.
